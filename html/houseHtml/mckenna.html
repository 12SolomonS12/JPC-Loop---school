About McKenna House

McKenna House is JPC’s blue house, named after Father Daniel McKenna. Father McKenna was a Mill Hill Missionary from Ireland who came to New Zealand after becoming a priest in 1939. He became the first parish priest of St Mary’s Parish in Rotorua and played an important role in developing Catholic education in the area, including helping establish MacKillop College and Edmund Rice College, which later became John Paul College. McKenna House carries this legacy through service, leadership, and involvement in school life.
