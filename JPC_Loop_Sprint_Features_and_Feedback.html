Product overview

What the sprint currently delivers

JPC Loop is a responsive school community website where students can view announcements, share posts, explore their house, browse school photos and sign in using a JPC account.

Core purpose

Bring JPC news, student discussion, house identity and school media into one recognisable online space.

Current technical state

IMPLEMENTED

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore are connected in the current code. Posts and homepage previews update in real time.

Typical user journey

1. ArriveHomepage hero and latest posts
2. ExploreFeed, News, Houses or Gallery
3. Sign inJPC email or Google
4. ParticipateCreate and manage posts
5. ReturnSee live community updates

Main sections

Home

School hero image, clear calls to action and three latest News and Feed previews.

Feed

Student community posts with title, description, author, timestamp and expandable cards.

News

School announcements. Only administrators are allowed to create news posts.

Houses

Rice, Kanea, La Salle, McKenna and MacKillop pages with information and individual feeds.

Gallery

Separate junior and senior image viewers with arrows and clickable thumbnails.

Guidelines

Eight clear expectations covering respect, safety, privacy, relevance and responsible posting.

Accounts and data

Authentication, roles and Firebase

Account creation and sign-in

Email + passwordGoogle popupSign out

Only addresses ending in @jpc.school.nz are accepted. Registration checks matching passwords and saves the display name.

Signed-in state

Firebase observes authentication changes. The sign-in form is replaced by the user’s name, role and a Sign out button. The display name is also cached locally.

Firestore user record

Each UID stores display name, email, role, creation time and last login. New accounts default to the user role.

Role behaviour

User: create normal Feed/house posts and delete their own. Admin: create News and delete any displayed post.

Live content

Firestore snapshot listeners refresh Feed, News, house feeds and homepage previews without a manual reload.

Data structure

Posts are grouped under pages / page-name / posts. Separate page names prevent Feed, News and each house from mixing.
